Company overview

Who we are

Misalta Global Concepts Ltd. is a Nigerian company established in 2019. It has provided professional services and is developing a broader business platform spanning climate-focused agribusiness, agro-industrial development, agricultural trade, and investment advisory.

The company's evolving strategy is to connect service delivery, productive infrastructure, commodity value chains, and market access to create practical opportunities for businesses and agricultural stakeholders.

Our journey

An evolution, step by step

2019: Established. Misalta Global Concepts Ltd. begins as a professional services company.
Professional services. Training, facility management and procurement support delivered to leading organisations in Nigeria, including NNPCL and NCDC.
Expanding focus. Planned ventures in agro-processing, agricultural infrastructure and digital commodity trading.
Looking ahead. Agro-processing in Taraba State, an agro-hub network, and the upcoming Misalta Trade X platform. These are planned or in development.
