About Misalta

What is Misalta Global Concepts Ltd.?

Misalta Global Concepts Ltd. is a Nigerian company established in 2019 (CAC RC 1571831). It is the parent company of eight businesses that grow, process, store, move and sell agricultural produce, supported by our own energy, construction and consulting teams. Read our story.

Where is Misalta based?

Our work is centred on Taraba State, with sites identified in Jalingo, Kurmi, Lau, Gashaka and Bali, and an office in Abuja. We serve clients and partners across Nigeria and plan to trade across Africa.

Who leads Misalta?

The group is led by our Founder & CEO, Samaila Bah (FMVA®, BIDA®, FIMC, CMC), with a board, directors, and financial, agricultural and legal advisers. Meet our people.

Is Misalta a registered company?

Yes. Misalta Global Concepts Ltd. is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission (RC 1571831) and holds tax, pension, social insurance, training-fund and federal contractor registrations, among others. The full list is on our Trust & Compliance page.

Our Businesses

What businesses are in the Misalta group?

Five along the agricultural value chain: Misalta Farms, Misalta Agro Processing, Misalta Agro Hubs, Misalta Logistics and Misalta Trade X. Three enablers support them: Misalta Energy, Misalta Construction and Misalta Consulting.

Which businesses are operating today?

Misalta Consulting has been operating since 2019, and Misalta Energy delivers oil and gas services through it. Farms and Agro Processing are in development, Trade X is launching soon, and the others are planned. Each page shows its current status, and we always say what is live and what is planned.

What do the status labels mean?

Operating: serving clients today. In development: being built now. Launching soon: opening next, with early access available. Planned: part of our roadmap, open to partners.

Will each business have its own website?

Yes. As each business grows it will get its own website, and this site will link to it. Misalta Global stays the front door for the whole group.

Investing & Partnerships

How can I invest in Misalta?

You can invest in the group as a whole or in a single business, through equity, project or asset finance, offtake agreements or programme partnerships. Send an investment enquiry and our team will share information suited to your mandate. See Invest & Partner.

What happens after I send an investment enquiry?

We review it, get in touch within two business days, share the relevant information and, where there is a fit, agree next steps and terms. Verified investors can request our compliance pack with certificate copies.

What kinds of partners are you looking for?

Development finance and impact investors, banks and agricultural lenders, farmer cooperatives and communities, offtakers and exporters, equipment and technology suppliers, and government and development programmes.

Do you give investment advice or guaranteed returns?

No. Information on this website is not investment advice and does not promise any return. Any investment is made on agreed terms after proper due diligence.

Misalta Trade X

What is Misalta Trade X?

A digital commodity marketplace that bridges the gap between farmers, buyers and traders, so farmers get a fair price for their produce and buyers get reliable supply. Learn more.

When does Trade X launch, and how do I join?

Trade X is launching soon. Join the early access list and we will contact you as the platform opens. Registration does not guarantee admission or trading access.

Which commodities will Trade X cover?

Grains, legumes and oilseeds, cash and export crops such as cocoa, cashew, sesame and ginger, palm produce, roots and tubers, fruits and vegetables, livestock and fish, and processed products.

How will Trade X help farmers get a fair price?

By connecting farmers directly with buyers and traders, with fewer layers in between and open prices, so farmers can see what their produce is worth and keep more of the value they grow.

You can partner with Misalta Farms on land and cultivation, sell to our processing and hubs, or join Trade X early access to reach more buyers at fair prices.
