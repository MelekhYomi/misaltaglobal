Our experience

Professional Excellence. Proven Experience. A Vision for the Future.

Misalta Global Ltd. has previously provided training services, facility management, and procurement support to leading organisations in Nigeria, including the Nigerian National Petroleum Company Limited (NNPCL) and the Nigeria Centre for Disease Control (NCDC). Building on this experience, the company is expanding its focus into climate-focused agribusiness, agro-industrial development, agricultural commodity trading, and investment advisory.
