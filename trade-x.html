Why Misalta Trade X

Bridging the Gap Between Farmers and Buyers

Too often, farmers sell through long chains of middlemen and receive a fraction of what their produce is worth, while buyers and traders struggle to find reliable supply. Trade X brings both sides together.

For the market

Open prices, traceable produce and connected logistics, storage and finance as the platform grows.
