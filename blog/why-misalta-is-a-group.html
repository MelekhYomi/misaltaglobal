Why Misalta is building a group, not a single business

Samaila BahFounder & Chief Executive Officer · October 2026

Farming, processing, storage, logistics and trade depend on each other. Building them together lets each business strengthen the next.

Many agricultural ventures fail not because the idea is wrong but because a link in the chain is missing. A processor without steady supply sits idle. A farmer without storage sells at harvest prices. A trader without transport cannot reach buyers.

Misalta is organised around the value chain: grow, add value, store, move and sell. Each business has its own customers and partners, but each also feeds the next. Farms supply processing, hubs hold produce until the right time to sell, logistics moves it and Trade X connects it to buyers.

Three enablers support the chain. Energy keeps sites running, construction builds the hubs and facilities, and consulting brings training, procurement and advisory experience that the group has delivered since 2019.

A group structure also gives partners choice. An investor can back the whole group or a single business that fits their mandate, and a buyer can work with one business while knowing the others stand behind it.

We will share progress on each business here as it moves from plan to operation, and we will always be clear about what is live and what is still being built.
