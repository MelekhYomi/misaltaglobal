Data protection basics for Nigerian businesses under the NDPA

Yomi DeleContributor · October 2026

The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 sets clear duties for any organisation that handles personal data. Here is where to start.

Almost every business handles personal data: customer names and phone numbers, staff records, supplier contacts. The Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 sets out how that data must be collected, used and protected, and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission enforces it.

Start by knowing what you hold. List the personal data you collect, why you collect it, where it is stored and who can access it. You cannot protect what you have not mapped.

Next, have a lawful reason for each use, such as consent or a contract, and tell people clearly how you use their data in a privacy notice. Collect only what you need and keep it only as long as you need it.

Protect the data with sensible security: access controls, strong passwords, updated software and backups. Train staff, because many breaches start with a simple mistake.

Larger organisations may need to register with the Commission, appoint a data protection officer and file annual compliance audits. Misalta Consulting offers data protection support and training for organisations working through these requirements.
