Who we are

This website is run by Misalta Global Concepts Ltd., a company registered in Nigeria and the parent company of the Misalta businesses shown on this site. We are responsible for the information you send us here. For any privacy question, email info@misaltaglobal.com.

What we collect

Only what you choose to send us through a form on this website:

  • your name and email address;
  • optional details such as phone or WhatsApp number, organisation, state and country;
  • what you are interested in, such as the Misalta business, the type of enquiry, or a product and quantity; and
  • your message.

We do not ask for payment details, identity numbers or passwords through this website.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, pass it to the Misalta team that can help, and follow up on the request you made. If you registered for Misalta Trade X early access and agreed to be contacted, we may send you updates about the platform. Registering does not guarantee admission, trading access or a commercial relationship. We do not use your information for unrelated marketing without your consent.

How forms reach us

When you submit a form, your message is sent to the relevant Misalta inbox through Web3Forms, a form delivery service, which passes it on to us by email. If that service is unavailable, the form opens your own email app with the message prepared, and nothing is sent until you press send there.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell your information. We share it only within the Misalta group, with the business you asked about, and with service providers who help us run this website and deliver your messages. We keep enquiries only as long as we need them to respond and to keep a reasonable record of our business dealings, and then delete them.

Cookies, analytics and fonts

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

To understand which pages are useful, we may measure visits with Vercel Web Analytics. It does not use cookies, does not follow you across other websites, and gives us only totals, such as page views and how often an enquiry form is opened or sent. It never records what you type into a form.

Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can withdraw your consent to being contacted. Email info@misaltaglobal.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You also have rights under Nigerian data protection law, including the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our website and services change. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
